### Hello, I'm Pavol

Robotics & Cybernetics student focusing on **embedded firmware, hardware prototyping, and autonomous systems**. Alongside university coursework and IoT firmware development, I build game mods, server plugins, and community utilities under the handle **Spinkles**.

---

### Technical Stack

* **Languages:** C/C++, C# (.NET), Java, Python, MATLAB
* **Embedded & Wireless:** ESP32, Arduino, Bluetooth (BLE)
* **Hardware & CAD:** Fusion 360 (CAD, PCB Design), 3D Printing (FDM), Blender
* **Tools & Workflow:** Git, VS Code, Linux

---

### What I Built

* **Robotics & Autonomous Systems:** Custom two-wheeled self-balancing platforms, IMU/ultrasonic sensor integration, and custom PCBs designed from schematic to 3D-printed enclosure.
* **IoT & Embedded Firmware:** Low-power wireless telemetry, mesh networking, and RF packet parsing on microcontrollers.
* **Modding & Utilities (`Spinkles`):** Game modifications, custom multiplayer server frameworks, and lightweight developer/gaming tools.
